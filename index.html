Zimin Ran冉子民

Zimin Ran is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Technology Sydney, under the supervision of Prof. Linchao Zhu of Zhejiang University. Her research focuses on digital humans and multimodal large models. Rather than viewing AI as a purely academic pursuit, she is committed to translating cutting-edge AI into practical solutions for industry and real-world applications. Her interdisciplinary background enables her to combine strong technical vision and judgment with sound business sense, a solid foundation in finance, clear risk awareness, and an informed understanding of legal and compliance frameworks.

冉子民，悉尼科技大学计算机科学博士研究生，师从浙江大学朱霖潮教授。研究方向为数字人与多模态大模型。她不将 AI 视为纯粹的学术研究，而是致力于将前沿 AI 技术转化为产业和现实场景中的实际解决方案。她的跨学科背景使她兼具技术视野与判断力、扎实的金融基础、良好的商业敏感度、清晰的风险意识，以及对法律合规框架的深入理解。
