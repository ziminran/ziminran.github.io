Research trajectory研究主线

From visual representations to open multimodal intelligence从视觉表征
到开放多模态智能

My research connects a central question across scales: how can machines learn meaningful visual structure, perceive people reliably, and turn image and video understanding into accessible multimodal systems?

我的研究围绕一个逐层展开的问题：如何让机器学到有意义的视觉结构，可靠地感知人，并将图像与视频理解转化为开放、可复现的多模态系统？

Learn the structure of the visual world学到视觉世界的内在结构

RICE

Region-based Cluster Discrimination for Visual Representation Learning

Region-based cluster discrimination brings object and OCR semantics into a shared learning framework, strengthening the local visual representations needed for dense prediction and multimodal perception.通过区域级聚类判别，在统一框架中学习物体与 OCR 语义，增强密集预测与多模态感知所需的局部视觉表征。

02

Make human perception reliable让对人的感知更可靠

At the human scale, meaningful representations must handle variation. Two complementary directions address appearance under changing illumination and identity across changing poses.将表征问题聚焦到人：面对真实场景中的变化，如何保持感知的可靠性？两条互补方向分别关注光照变化下的外观与姿态变化下的身份。

▶04:48
ICCV 2025Appearance & fairness外观与公平性

HUST

High-Fidelity Unbiased Skin Tone Estimation via Texture Quantization

Texture quantization and texture-to-albedo adaptation recover high-fidelity facial albedo from a single image, reducing illumination effects and skin tone bias for realistic digital humans.通过纹理量化与纹理到反照率的域适配，从单张图像恢复高保真人脸反照率，减少光照干扰与肤色偏差，为真实数字人提供外观基础。

CVPRW 2026Identity & pose身份与姿态

NPTFace

Native Pose-aligned Transformer for Face Recognition

Native pose alignment within a Transformer addresses pose variation in face recognition, exploring how identity representations can remain stable when facial geometry changes.在 Transformer 中引入原生姿态对齐，缓解人脸识别中的姿态变化问题，探索如何在面部几何变化时仍保持稳定的身份表征。

03

Connect still images and a moving world从静态图像走向动态世界

Broaden the question from specialized perception to general visual understanding: can one encoder represent both what appears in an image and what happens over time?从具体感知任务进一步走向通用视觉理解：能否用同一个编码器，既理解图像中的内容，也理解视频中随时间发生的事件？

▶03:30
NeurIPS 2025Unified spatiotemporal representations统一时空表征

UniViT

Unifying Image and Video Understanding in One Vision Encoder

A single vision encoder unifies image and video learning through object- and event-level clustering and unified positional encoding, connecting spatial semantics with temporal dynamics.通过物体级与事件级聚类及统一位置编码，在单一视觉编码器中联合学习图像与视频，连接空间语义与时间动态。

04

Turn visual capability into open training从视觉能力走向开放训练体系

Beyond the encoder, building a multimodal system also requires accessible data, training recipes, and reproducible experiments.有了视觉理解能力，还需要开放的数据、训练方法与可复现的实验流程，才能构建可持续迭代的多模态系统。

Looking ahead未来研究方向

Human-centered multimodal intelligence面向人的多模态智能

My next research direction is to connect fine-grained human perception, temporal understanding, and language interaction in digital humans and multimodal assistants, with reliability, fairness, and reproducibility as guiding principles.下一步，我希望在数字人与多模态助手中进一步连接细粒度人体感知、时序理解与语言交互，并将可靠性、公平性与可复现性贯穿其中。

02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE RESEARCH研究背后的我

Curiosity, across disciplines.跨越学科，保持好奇。

Zimin Ran holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Technology Sydney, under the supervision of Prof. Linchao Zhu (formerly Assistant Professor at UTS, now Professor at Zhejiang University). Her research focuses on digital humans and multimodal large models. Rather than viewing AI as a purely academic pursuit, she is committed to translating cutting-edge AI into practical solutions for industry and real-world applications. Her interdisciplinary background enables her to combine strong technical vision and judgment with sound business sense, a solid foundation in finance, clear risk awareness, and an informed understanding of legal and compliance frameworks.

冉子民，悉尼科技大学计算机科学博士，师从朱霖潮教授（原 UTS 助理教授，现浙江大学教授）。研究方向为数字人与多模态大模型。她不将 AI 视为纯粹的学术研究，而是致力于将前沿 AI 技术转化为产业和现实场景中的实际解决方案。她的跨学科背景使她兼具技术视野与判断力、扎实的金融基础、良好的商业敏感度、清晰的风险意识，以及对法律合规框架的深入理解。
